Paper Submission

All paper submissions for KGMA 2026 will be via Microsoft CMT (under the 9th International Workshop on Knowledge Graph Management and Applications (KGMA 2026)).

https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/APWebWAIM2026

All submissions must be written in English and conform to the Springer LNCS proceedings format. Please use one of the following templates for the LNCS (Lecture Notes in Computer Science) format: (https://www.springer.com/gp/computer-science/lncs/conference-proceedings-guidelines).

Authors must provide the complete and final list of authors at the submission stage. No addition, removal, or change in the order of authors is allowed after submission. Submissions must be original (not previously published and not under review in other forums). Each accepted paper must be accompanied by at least one full registration, and an author is expected to present the paper at the conference, otherwise, the paper will be removed from the proceedings.

The maximal length of the paper is 12 pages including references.
